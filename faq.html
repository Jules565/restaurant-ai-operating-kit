FAQ

Everything you need to know.

Clear answers before you decide whether the kit fits your restaurant.

What is Restaurant AI Operating Kit?

A digital toolkit containing 50 workflows, 7 specialized AI assistants, 15 templates, a 30-day content planner, a quick prompt cheat sheet and a Start Here guide.

Is this a subscription?

No. The intended price is €14.99 as a one-time purchase. There is no recurring subscription fee.

Do I need ChatGPT Plus?

No. The workflows are designed to work with ChatGPT and Claude. The limits and features of the AI provider you use still apply.

Does it work with the free version of ChatGPT?

The workflows themselves do not require a special integration. You can use them with a compatible ChatGPT account, subject to the provider's current limits.

Does it work with Claude?

Yes. The product is designed around prompts and workflows that can be adapted to Claude as well as ChatGPT.

Do I need AI experience?

No. The kit is designed to give you a structured starting point instead of requiring you to know how to write prompts from scratch.

Is this suitable for cafés?

Yes. The workflows can be adapted to cafés and other hospitality businesses with similar marketing, customer and operational needs.

Is this suitable for small restaurants?

Yes. The workflows are designed around common restaurant tasks rather than a specific company size.

How do I receive the product?

The intended experience is instant digital delivery after successful payment. The actual delivery provider will be connected before launch.

Is the product downloadable?

Yes, the final product is intended to be delivered as digital files. The exact file format and delivery mechanism will be configured before launch.

Can I use the workflows multiple times?

The kit is designed for repeated use. You can adapt a workflow to different tasks and situations.

Does the kit automatically post on social media?

No. It helps you create and structure content. You decide what to publish and where.

Does it guarantee more customers or revenue?

No. AI tools cannot guarantee business outcomes. The kit is designed to help you work more systematically; results depend on how you use the outputs and your business context.

Can I use it on multiple restaurants?

What happens after payment?

The intended flow is payment → confirmation → secure digital delivery. The payment and delivery providers will be connected before the public launch.
